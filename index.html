About us

Built by the people who used to get paged at 3am.

Nexbyte Solutions started in 2019 when three sysadmins got tired of watching small businesses get burned by IT contractors who never picked up the phone. We do things differently: one dedicated point of contact, plain-language explanations, and a monitoring stack that catches problems before your team notices anything's wrong.

Today we manage networks, cloud infrastructure, and day-to-day support for companies that would rather focus on their own customers than chase down a ticket queue.
